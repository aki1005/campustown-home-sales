College-Town Home Sales Explorer

Compare median prices, campus-area trends, and time on market across the dataset.

How to read these numbers: Q1–Q5 use every sale in the dataset whose sale price is from $10,000 through $5,000,000, inclusive. Each listing is counted once using listing_id; repeated street addresses are not deduplicated. Days on market are contract_date minus list_date, and only rows with both dates are used.
